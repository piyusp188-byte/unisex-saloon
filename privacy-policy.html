Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Amarr Salon ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle information received via amarrsalons.com and direct communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out our online appointment enquiry form, send us a WhatsApp message, or call our salon desk, we may collect your name, phone number, and appointment notes. We do not process online credit card transactions on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any details provided are solely used to schedule your appointment, answer your style queries, or provide directions to our salon at Sarthi Avenue Complex, Satellite, Ahmedabad. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses local storage purely to remember your preferred dark/light visual theme. Aggregated analytics data may be collected to understand general website traffic and improve local search accessibility.

For any questions regarding your data or this policy, please reach out to Amarr Salon at +91 99250 44343 or visit us at Shop No. 4/5, Sarthi Avenue Complex, Le Meridien Hotel Lane, Ramdevnagar, Satellite, Ahmedabad 380015.
