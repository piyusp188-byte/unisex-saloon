About Amarr Salon — Unisex Hair Salon in Satellite, Ahmedabad

Founded on the belief that a visit to the salon should be a calm, rejuvenating retreat rather than a rushed chore.

Located in the accessible Sarthi Avenue Complex along Le Meridien Hotel Lane in Ramdevnagar, Satellite, Amarr Salon has earned a 4.7-star rating across 486 Google reviews from discerning clients who appreciate genuine craftsmanship and attentive service.

Whether you are visiting for a sharp fade haircut, precision beard grooming, dimensional Ash Brown Balayage, or a stress-relieving head massage, our mission is to listen, advise, and deliver consistent quality in a space that feels like a quiet sanctuary in Ahmedabad.

Our Standards

What Sets Amarr Salon Apart

The qualities our customers repeatedly praise in verified Google feedback.

1. Spotless Cleanliness

Hygiene is non-negotiable. Every hair styling station, clipper guard, pair of shears, and wash basin is meticulously cleaned and sterilized between clients.

2. Calming Ambience

Our salon is intentionally styled with soothing warm cove lighting, ergonomic seating, and gentle acoustics to create a relaxed, stress-free space.

3. Polite & Attentive

We respect your time and preferences. Our stylists listen attentively without aggressive upselling, ensuring you feel genuinely heard and cared for.

4. Face-Shape Tailoring

We believe every face is unique. Instead of forcing cookie-cutter internet trends, we help you find the proportions that accentuate your best facial features.
